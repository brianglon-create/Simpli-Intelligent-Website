SCHEDULE A — SIMPLI CHECK PRODUCT TERMS
Version: v1.1 (final draft for counsel review) - November 22, 2026
Applies with the Master SaaS Terms to subscriptions for Simpli Check. When Customer also subscribes to Simpli Archive, Schedule B applies to Simpli Archive.
1. Product and professional responsibility
Simpli Check supports inspection workflows, narratives, photographs and report creation. Polaris does not inspect properties or certify conditions. The inspector or Customer is responsible for inspection scope, observation, licensing, professional standards, report accuracy, client agreements and final approval. Simpli Assist suggestions require human review and acceptance; no generated observation should be treated as a verified defect without inspection evidence.
2. Pricing and user licenses
Simpli Check is priced per user. Each person Customer authorizes to use Simpli Check requires a paid user license at the price stated in the order; current Early Adopter pricing is $49 per user per month. Every user, including account owners, inspectors and office or administrative staff, counts as a paid user. There are no separate or no-charge office or administrator seat types. Any exception is at Polaris's discretion, decided case by case, and applies only if confirmed in writing. User licenses are individual: sharing credentials or using one license for multiple people is prohibited.
3. Adding, pausing and removing users
The account owner may pause or remove a user's access in the Service at any time. Pausing or removing a user does not by itself change billing; to reduce the number of paid users, Customer must also email Hello@simpliintelligent.com, and the reduction takes effect at the end of the current billing period as described in the Master SaaS Terms.
4. Published report allowance
Simpli Check is intended for ordinary professional inspection activity. Polaris may monitor for unusually high publication volume, automated publication, account sharing or other misuse and may temporarily restrict new publication, investigate activity, or authorize an exception. Revisions to an existing report are not treated as new inspections for reasonable-use review. These safeguards do not delete existing reports.
5. Simpli Assist and AI processing
Simpli Assist uses AI to suggest narratives, analyze photographs and read equipment data plates. Relevant text and photographs are sent to Polaris's third-party AI provider, currently OpenAI, to perform these functions. Under the provider's API terms and Polaris's account settings, this content is not used to train the provider's general-purpose models, and the provider's standard data-retention practices apply. Simpli Assist is subject to reasonable-use safeguards, including request-rate, concurrency and daily usage controls intended to preserve reliability and prevent automated abuse. Requests may be queued, declined or temporarily restricted when safeguards are triggered, and Polaris may review and override restrictions for legitimate use. Temporary Simpli Assist working data, such as suggestions that are not accepted, may expire and be removed automatically. Existing inspection records are not deleted because a safeguard is triggered.
6. Historical narratives and imports
Customer retains its rights in imported reports and narratives and must have permission to import them. Import and mapping tools may misclassify or imperfectly match historical narratives to observation categories; Customer must review results and accepted defaults. Source files and narrative wording are not intentionally rewritten as part of mapping. Customer should retain independent copies of original historical materials.
7. Photographs and report media
Customer retains its rights in uploaded photographs and is responsible for their accuracy and lawful use. Editing tools may create annotated copies. Simpli Check is designed to retain published report PDFs and their report photographs for at least seven years, and saved reference photographs for at least one year after report finalization, and to prevent their early deletion during those periods. These are minimum retention designs, not a promise of indefinite hosting. Customer must keep its own copies to meet professional record-retention obligations.
8. Exports
Supported exports include CSV exports of business records, PDF downloads of individual reports and signed documents, and downloads of photographs and other media. Simpli Check does not provide a single full-account export package. Customer may request historical and migration data by contacting Hello@simpliintelligent.com.
9. Client agreements and delivery
Inspection contracts are between Customer and its clients; Polaris is not a party to them. Customer is responsible for legally sufficient agreements, required notices, signature validity and report recipients. The availability of an electronic-signature feature does not guarantee legal sufficiency in every use case.
10. Failed payment and access after cancellation
If a payment fails, the three-day notice and pause terms in the Master SaaS Terms apply; while paused, report writing is locked and historical data remains available in read-only form. After a cancellation takes effect, Simpli Check becomes read-only: existing records can be viewed and downloaded through supported functions, and new reports cannot be created or published. Polaris does not automatically delete Simpli Check records when a subscription ends. Customer may request historical and migration data by contacting Hello@simpliintelligent.com.
11. Professional disclaimers
AI, templates, suggested narratives and equipment data-plate recognition are assistance only, not building-code certification, legal advice, engineering opinion or inspection services. These disclaimers do not waive Polaris's own express software obligations.
