1. Scope and roles
This Policy describes personal information processed through Simpli Intelligent's website, Simpli Check, Simpli Archive and business operations. For subscriber accounts, billing, sales, marketing and security, Polaris generally determines the purposes of processing. For reports, photographs, client information and documents uploaded by business customers, Polaris generally processes information on the business customer's instructions. Individuals whose information appears in a customer's records should usually contact that business first; Polaris will assist as legally required.
2. Information collected
Categories may include identifiers (names, business names, emails, phone numbers, IP addresses, property addresses); account roles and authentication records; subscription, payment status and transaction information (card details are handled by our payment processor, not stored by us); records of agreement acceptance, including typed names, date and time, IP address and browser information; device, session, usage and diagnostic information; anti-abuse signals, which may include hashed IP addresses and email addresses; support correspondence; lead-form information (business, product interest, current software, marketing-consent choice and submitted details); inspection reports, narratives, equipment information, photographs and related metadata; archived files and extracted or indexed text; and AI requests and outputs needed to operate requested features. Some customer-uploaded records may contain sensitive information. Sources include users, business customers, automated systems and our service providers.
3. Purposes
We use information to provide and secure accounts and Services; store, index, search, create and deliver requested records; process payments and subscriptions; record agreement acceptance; enforce user-license and usage restrictions; respond to sales inquiries, support and privacy requests; diagnose errors and prevent fraud and abuse; send operational notices and, with consent where required, marketing; comply with law; and improve reliability using information we are authorized to use.

5. AI processing
When customers use AI features such as Simpli Assist, relevant text, photographs and outputs are sent to our AI provider, currently OpenAI, to generate narrative suggestions, analyze photographs and read equipment data plates. Under OpenAI's API terms and our account settings, this content is not used to train OpenAI's general-purpose models. OpenAI's standard data-retention practices apply; we do not use a zero-data-retention arrangement. We do not authorize general-purpose AI model training on customer-uploaded content without the customer's separate authorization. AI outputs can be inaccurate and require customer review. We may also use AI tools to research and organize sales inquiries submitted to us.
6. Sales, targeted advertising and cookies
We do not sell personal information, and we do not use customer-uploaded inspection reports or archived documents for targeted advertising. The Simpli Check application uses cookies that are necessary for it to work: Supabase authentication cookies that keep users signed in securely, and a cookie (sc_active_company) that remembers the selected company for 30 days. Our hosting and security providers may also set cookies necessary for security and operation, such as bot protection on forms. We may use website analytics in the future; if we use analytics or advertising technologies, we will update this Policy and provide any notices, choices and opt-outs required by law.
7. Security
We apply reasonable administrative, technical and organizational measures designed to protect personal information, including access restrictions appropriate to the Services. No system can guarantee complete security.
8. Retention
We retain information as reasonably needed to provide the Services, administer accounts, meet legal obligations and resolve disputes. When a subscription ends, Simpli Check becomes read-only and Simpli Archive remains viewable, searchable and downloadable without new uploads. We do not automatically delete customer records when a subscription ends. Customers may request historical and migration data at Hello@simpliintelligent.com. Product-specific retention is described in the Data Access, Retention & Deletion Policy. Our database provider maintains backups under its own terms; we do not promise a particular backup period.

Requests are subject to verification, lawful exceptions and the respective roles of Polaris and its business customers.
